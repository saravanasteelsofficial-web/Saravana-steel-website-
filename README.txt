HOW ORDERS WORK
Customers choose products, add quantities to the cart, enter their name/mobile/address and tap “Send Order on WhatsApp”. The site opens WhatsApp with the full order details pre-filled to your business number. The customer must press Send in WhatsApp. The website does not automatically send a message in the background and does not collect online payments.

PRODUCT CATALOG
The initial product list is a starter list with generic categories/brands. To change it, edit the PRODUCTS array near the bottom of index.html. Prices are not displayed because current prices vary; the order asks your team to confirm rates and availability.

PUBLISHING
Upload index.html and saravana-steel-logo.png to your GitHub Pages repository (keep both in the same folder). Commit the changes and wait for GitHub Pages to redeploy.
